# AR-0041: Batched discussion packet TUI

Implement packet construction and rendering for list/tree navigation. Preserve
per-point status and response identity, expose candidate implications in a
helper window, and keep the session usable when only some points are answered.
Batch only dependency-independent points; coupled points declare their relation.

Add synthetic and hostile traces for omitted proposals, cross-point
authorization, user-added proposals that were not evaluated, and excessive
querying caused by failing to batch.
