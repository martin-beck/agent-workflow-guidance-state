# AR-0044: AWG-owned cross-project TUI test harness

Place all cross-project synthetic tests in the AWG product so the reusable TUI
can be validated independently of Coordinator and AWQ implementation details.
Use stable contract fixtures and adapters for the public event/evidence
boundaries. Keep the harness offline, deterministic, privacy-safe, and explicit
about which behavior is simulated versus actually executed.

Acceptance requires positive end-to-end traces and hostile traces for every
required gate and persistence invariant, exact contract-version references,
formal-check evidence, and independent review. Coordinator and AWQ retain
their own unit and policy tests; AR-0044 is the cross-project composition layer.
