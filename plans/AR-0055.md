# AR-0055: Remote endpoint advertisement contract

Specify the public-safe endpoint candidate envelope consumed by UI clients.
Candidates include DNS names, IPv4/IPv6 addresses, VPN/relay provenance,
ports, host-key fingerprints, expiry, and required phone-side validation. The
contract must preserve the distinction between service-side reachability and
phone-side reachability.

Acceptance includes canonical serialization, revision binding, redaction, and
hostile fixtures for replay, candidate substitution, and stale advertisements.
