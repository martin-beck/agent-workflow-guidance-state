AR must produce a successor specification for AR authoring that rejects a new
design/conceptual AR unless it includes a specification reference, exact
specification digest, passed autonomous-check result, checker limitations, and
the Coordinator task revision to which the evidence is bound. Operational ARs
must still declare a bounded specification, but may use a lighter method.
