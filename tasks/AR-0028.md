After AR-0021, AR-0022, AR-0023, and AR-0027 are complete, AWG must use its
own workflow for its own evolution:

- every AWG design or conceptual change gets an AWG specification;
- the specification is autonomously checked before an oracle packet is
  presented and again before implementation;
- alternatives, confidence, consequences, and oracle authority are recorded;
- Coordinator owns the AWG task, dependency, revision, claim, and transition
  state;
- AWQ checks the applicable quality, privacy, schema, and formal-evidence
  contracts;
- implementation, formal-model, quality, CI, and release evidence remain
  separate from oracle approval.

The self-hosting specification must define the recursion boundary: the
bootstrap specification and checker are the trusted initial seed; any change
to that seed, the formal checker, or this self-hosting rule requires a new
successor specification and autonomous check. Routine non-design maintenance
still requires bounded specifications but may use the operational method.

Completion requires a documented self-hosting policy, an exact Coordinator
transition that enables it, a negative test showing an un-specified AWG change
is rejected, and a passed autonomous formal-check result.
