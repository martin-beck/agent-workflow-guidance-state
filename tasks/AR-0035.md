This is a design/conceptual AR. Define mandatory interaction-gate AR types for
initial intake/work-plan/design review, user-requested discussion, formal
specification review, and post-discussion reconciliation. Every gate must have
versioned before/after work-plan and design-document references, specification
and formal-check references, and an explicit unresolved/continue decision.

The contract is consumed by Coordinator AR-0022 and AWQ AR-0058. A gate AR
cannot be silently completed when the user interaction, specification review,
or post-discussion reconciliation is absent.

Formal deliverable: `specifications/oracle-workflow-lifecycle.json` with an
autonomous result and a checker that rejects missing interaction gates,
missing before/after artifacts, stale task revisions, and unresolved
contradictions marked complete.
