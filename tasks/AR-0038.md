After every discussion, the agent must analyze the guidance, update the work
plan, design document, specifications, and affected ARs, and record before and
after versions. Contradictory, incomplete, or stale guidance must reopen only
the affected ARs and trigger another discussion on those exact points. A
discussion cannot be marked complete while unresolved next steps remain.

Formal deliverable: a checked reconciliation state machine with contradiction,
scope-change, and repeated-discussion fixtures.
