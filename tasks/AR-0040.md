The AWG product owns a reusable discussion TUI for agent-initiated and
user-initiated discussions in any planned project. The UI has a left pane that
can live-switch between the current concise design document and work plan, and
a right list/tree of open discussion points. Navigation must update the left
anchor, scroll position, and phrase/key-word highlighting; unresolved points
remain visibly highlighted.

This is a design/conceptual AR. Formal deliverable:
`specifications/discussion-tui-navigation.json` and an autonomous checker for
the two-pane, anchor, synchronization, and unresolved-point invariants.
