Safe exit must durably store all proposals, evaluations, selections, rejections,
clarifications, unresolved points, and session revisions in the planned
project's AR records. A point may be marked for a later re-ask without losing
its current state. The final free-text request for further discussion must be
classified and mapped explicitly to existing or new ARs; it may never be
dropped or treated as an implementation instruction implicitly.

Formal deliverable: `specifications/discussion-persistence.json` and an
autonomous crash/restart checker covering atomic save, resume, re-ask, and
future-AR mapping.
