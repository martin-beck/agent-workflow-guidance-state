The AWG product repository is the authoritative home for cross-project tests
covering the reusable TUI and its Coordinator/AWQ contracts. Add deterministic
synthetic fixtures and an offline harness for agent- and user-initiated entry,
two-pane synchronization, batching, per-point and user-authored proposals,
safe exit, resume/re-ask, future-request-to-AR mapping, reconciliation, and
quality/task handoff.

The harness must consume versioned public contracts from Coordinator AR-0029
and AWQ AR-0065 without importing their implementation internals. It must
include hostile traces for skipped interaction/specification gates, stale
revisions, cross-point authorization, dropped future requests, and quality
evidence being mistaken for user intent.

Formal deliverable: `specifications/cross-project-tui-tests.json` with an
autonomous checker and explicit limitations. This AR owns test orchestration
only; Coordinator remains task-state authority and AWQ remains quality-policy
authority.
