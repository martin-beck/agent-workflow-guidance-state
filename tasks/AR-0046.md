When no more independent ARs can run, Coordinator must persist the exact
human batch, its AR revisions, session identity, and waiting reason. The agent
frontend must be able to render a concise handoff card and resume polling
without treating a displayed prompt as an accepted decision. Partial,
clarify, rejected, disconnected, and completed states remain distinguishable.

Acceptance requires revision/CAS tests, restart recovery tests, and a public
agent-facing status contract.
