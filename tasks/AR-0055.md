Define the versioned contract by which a Coordinator/UI service advertises
endpoint candidates, provenance, expiry, address family, host-key fingerprints,
and phone-side probe requirements. It must not contain private keys, secrets,
private paths, or unredacted agent prompts.

Acceptance requires canonical schema fixtures, revision/CAS binding, expiry,
redaction, split-DNS candidates, and deterministic candidate ordering.
