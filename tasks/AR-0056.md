Define the authorization contract for phone-generated public keys, explicit
user consent, one-time enrollment, restricted forwarding, host-key validation,
rotation, revocation, reconnect, and fail-closed transport downgrade. QR
payloads must never carry private keys or reusable credentials.

Acceptance requires positive and hostile fixtures for key substitution, QR
capture/replay, wrong host keys, expiry, revocation, and tunnel scope escape.
